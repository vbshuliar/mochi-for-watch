## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a photorealistic premium 3D studio product render of one Apple Watch SE 44mm in starlight aluminum with a warm ivory sport band. This is a website hero asset, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Portrait canvas. The watch floats upright, band curving naturally behind the case into an open loop, visible inner band and elegant depth. The camera looks straight at the display: screen edges horizontal/vertical, NO rotation or perspective distortion on the display plane so a live HTML UI can be composited exactly. Add depth through realistic curved aluminum sidewalls, physically accurate knurled Digital Crown and side button on the right, curved polished glass rim, soft studio highlights and realistic silicone material. The band curves backward in depth instead of lying flat, creating a sculptural three-dimensional silhouette. The center display must be completely plain solid black, with NO text, icons, clock, character, or UI, and minimal reflection in its center; bezel highlights only. Entire watch and strap visible with generous transparent margin. Crisp high quality luxury commercial product photography, restrained warm neutral tones, convincing materials and realistic Apple Watch proportions. No logos, no watermark, no extra objects, no stand. Prefer 1024 x 1536 portrait.
