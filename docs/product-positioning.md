# Mochi product positioning

Research reviewed 30 October 2026. This is a product strategy, not measured market validation.

## Position

**Your AI secretary. On the Apple Watch you already own.**

Audience: existing Apple Watch owners who want quick answers and help with everyday administration, without adopting another wearable. The initial prototype additionally requires a technically capable owner to set up a backend and install from source; consumer distribution is a future step, not a current promise.

Consumer benefit: reduce the effort between thinking of something and dealing with it. Questions, memory, reminders and delegated work belong to one experience. A friendly character makes repeated interactions feel familiar. Autonomous booking demonstrates the depth of the product; it does not define the whole category.

## Market evidence and implications

| [Bee](https://bee.computer/) | Personal wearable AI with conversation summaries and insights, including a wrist/clothing form factor. | Interest in frequent, accessible assistance extends beyond phone chat windows. This is evidence of product activity, not proof of consumer demand or saturation. |
| [Omi](https://www.omi.me/) and its [product overview](https://help.omi.me/en/articles/13135007-overview) | Conversation capture, memories and tasks across devices. | Memory alone is not a unique differentiator. Make useful follow-through tangible. |
| [Muse Charm announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/) | A dedicated pocket device for voice interaction with an agent. | Existing Watch hardware is a credible alternative starting point for people who already own one. Do not claim price, battery, shipping or superiority without verified evidence. |
| [Moment](https://momentdevices.com/) | Agents on Apple Watch, including talking, delegating work and meeting notes. | Do not claim Mochi is the first or only agent on Apple Watch. Differentiate the demonstrated experience, transparent boundaries and inspectable source. |

The market hypothesis is that a device people already wear reduces an additional hardware adoption step. We have not measured demand, conversion, time saved, retention, battery impact or willingness to pay. Avoid numerical savings and absolute claims about competing products.

## Hackathon fit

The [official event](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon) and [organizer page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon) describe Build for Your World around practical problems and time/mental-space benefits. The indie track emphasizes creativity, technical ambition and working execution.

Lead the demo with an ordinary human need, show a quick conversation, then delegate a concrete task such as the haircut call. Make approval, autonomous conversation and the final verified result visible. A short path from intent to an inspectable outcome is more convincing than a catalogue of integrations. Model-powered understanding and tool planning should be demonstrated in the product, with technical evidence linked for reviewers.

This positioning is an interpretation of the event themes, not a guarantee of eligibility or judging results. Full live-call acceptance remains pending. Use a consenting demo recipient and retain separate Calendar approval. Gmail and Notion remain outside the signature demo.

## Messaging guardrails

- Say AI secretary or personal AI companion; use autonomous for carrying out approved tasks within supported workflows.
- Say on the Watch you already own or close at hand. Do not imply always-on listening, offline intelligence or uninterrupted availability.
- Show conversation, memory and delegation before installation and architecture.
- Treat the haircut as a compelling example, not the product category.
- Use actual interface captures. Identify website captures versus physical-device evidence in asset documentation.
- Explain prototype availability honestly at the point where someone would try or install it.
- Use competitor names only for sourced market context, with no borrowed logos or character art and no endorsement implication.
