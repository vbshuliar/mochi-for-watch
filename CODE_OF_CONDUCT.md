# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination. Respect privacy and consent, especially around voice recordings, contact details and connected accounts.

Do not use this project to place unwanted calls, impersonate people, expose private information or bypass service permissions. Use synthetic examples in public reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an established channel; do not publish sensitive incident details. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
